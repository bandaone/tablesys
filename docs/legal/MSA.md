# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Between:**

**TABLESYS,** a company registered in the Republic of Zambia

**[University Name]** ("Customer")

## 1. Services
TABLESYS provides a cloud-based timetable generation and management platform as described in the applicable Order Form and Service Level Agreement. The platform includes:
* Automated class timetable generation
* Exam scheduling
* Venue booking management
* Student and lecturer access portals
* Data export capabilities

## 2. Term and Termination
* **Initial Term:** 12 months from the Effective Date, unless otherwise stated in the Order Form
* **Auto-Renewal:** The agreement renews for successive 12-month periods unless either party provides 30 days' written notice before the end of the current term
* **Termination for Cause:** Either party may terminate immediately if the other materially breaches the agreement and fails to cure within 30 days of written notice
* **Termination for Convenience:** The Customer may terminate at any time with 30 days' written notice; prepaid fees for the remainder of the term are non-refundable

## 3. Fees and Payment
* Fees are as specified in the Order Form or the published pricing page at time of subscription
* Invoices are issued monthly or annually as selected by the Customer
* Payment is due within 30 days of invoice date
* Late payments accrue interest at 2% per month
* All fees are exclusive of VAT, which shall be added at the prevailing rate (currently 16% in Zambia)

## 4. Intellectual Property
* TABLESYS retains all rights, title, and interest in the platform, including the CP-SAT solver, algorithms, and all underlying code
* The Customer retains all rights to its data (courses, rooms, timetables, lecturer information)
* TABLESYS may use aggregated, anonymized data for product improvement and benchmarking; such data will never identify the Customer or any individual

## 5. Confidentiality
Each party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of the other's proprietary information for the term of this agreement and for 3 years thereafter. Confidential information includes, but is not limited to:
* The Customer's academic data and scheduling configurations
* TABLESYS source code, algorithms, and pricing structures

## 6. Limitation of Liability
* TABLESYS's total liability for any claim arising from this agreement shall not exceed the fees paid by the Customer in the 12 months preceding the claim
* Neither party shall be liable for indirect, consequential, or punitive damages
* The Customer acknowledges that timetable generation involves complex constraint satisfaction and that minor manual adjustments may occasionally be required

## 7. Warranties
TABLESYS warrants that:
* The platform will perform substantially as described in the documentation
* The platform does not infringe any third-party intellectual property rights
* All data will be stored and processed in accordance with the Data Processing Agreement

The Customer warrants that:
* It has the authority to upload all data provided to the platform
* It will not use the platform for any unlawful purpose

## 8. Data Protection
The parties agree to comply with the Zambia Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021. The Data Processing Agreement attached hereto forms an integral part of this MSA.

## 9. Governing Law
This agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Zambia. Disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Lusaka.
